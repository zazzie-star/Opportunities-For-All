# Opportunities-For-All

the premise:
- the core concept this website is that it allows anyone to browse (i hope!) hundreds of oppotunities that are availble to them without having to scour the internet. ("opportunities for all" - for all your linkdin-maxxing needs!)

- people upload interesting opportunities they know of, that maybe they've tried, or the actual companies behind these opportunities post opportunies on this forum

- then users can search for opportunies & use filters to find out whats availble to them. they could find hackathons, or online courses, or clubs & events - all sorts! sort by age, gender, location, kind of opportunity etc. using filters & find things you really want to try.

- the idea for this website came about from my desperate search for more opportunities online. 

- over the last year i've come to realise how many opportunies there are out there that could have helped me grow and learn that i missed out on and now can't do. 

- and, as someone who also just joined hack club and ages out very soon i found myself more and more disheartened that i have to leave such a wonderful community and all the support ive had, and i'm sure many other soon-to-be alums and alumni feel the same and are wondering what else is out there. 

- that's where my website comes in, "Opportunities For All". there's no age limit. find whatever opportunity you'd like and take as many as you can! this is your chance

- my goal is to make sure that no kid, teen, young adult & beyond, misses out on doing something exciting or getting the opportunty to learn and do things they otherwise couldn't just simply because they didn't know it existed, and i hope this website can succeed in that mission.
